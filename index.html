The philosophy

A way of building software

Localhost is the name a computer uses for itself. Anything running there stays on your machine.

I think AI is heading toward per-request pricing for everything, at prices most people won't keep paying. I'd rather the software run on the computer you already own.

The AI makes one small decision at a time, and ordinary code handles everything around it. A small decision is easy to check, and checked decisions add up to a system that behaves the same way every time.

  1. Is this a full sentence?yes
  2. Which language is it?Korean
  3. Translate into?English
  4. Show it now?yes

Example: one caption, 0 of 4 checked

local-host is how I build things, not only the name on them. Every project here follows the same rules:

  • It runs on your computer.
  • You own it, and nothing is metered.
  • The AI only makes decisions small enough to check.

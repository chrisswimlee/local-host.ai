Connecting Captions

Live captions on a Mac. Each sentence appears when it is ready. Use it for a presentation, a bilingual room, or any conversation that needs a translation.

Completely on-device by default. Speech recognition and Apple Translation run on this Mac. Voice never leaves the machine unless someone turns on a cloud speech model. Theater doesn’t call home. No account. No telemetry.

Who it’s for

  • Presentations. Theater sits over the slides. A whole-screen share includes it. Share the slides window when the room should not see the captions.
  • Bilingual rooms. Speak from that list. The other side sees a caption or a translation.
  • Any conversation. Put the Mac in the room. Each sentence appears when it is ready. Voice stays on the machine.

Install

macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon. The zip is notarized. Speech Analyzer, the newer Apple Speech, needs macOS 26.

Download Connecting Captions
  1. Unzip Connecting-Captions.zip and drag Connecting Captions to Applications.
  2. Open Connecting Captions, then Theater. Allow the microphone, then press Listen.

What it is

  • A Theater window over slides. A whole-screen Zoom or Meet share includes it. Share the slides window when viewers should not see the captions.
  • Apple Translation on this Mac. Apple Speech is the default: Speech Analyzer on macOS 26, and the older Apple Speech before that.
  • The speech engine comes from FluidVoice. Whisper and other models are optional downloads in Voice Engine.
  • Voice stays on this Mac. A cloud speech model is something you turn on.
  • No account, no telemetry, no captions uploaded. Works offline once models are on disk.

What you need

  • macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon. Speech Analyzer needs macOS 26.
  • Microphone for Listen. Accessibility only if you type into another app.

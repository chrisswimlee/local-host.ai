Connecting Captions

Privacy policy

Connecting Captions captions speech on this iPhone, iPad, or Mac. This page explains the little data that does move, and who handles it. It is run by Suyoung Lee (Chris Swim Lee), publishing as Local Host AI. Contact: support@local-host.ai.

Last updated 26 October 2026

On iPhone and iPad

  • The microphone is used only while Listen is on. Audio is not uploaded.
  • Speech recognition and translation run on the device with Apple’s frameworks.
  • The app does not collect an account, contacts, location, or usage analytics.
  • History, the dictionary, and talk notes stay on the device.
  • The keyboard asks for Full Access so it can read the last caption from this app. It cannot use the microphone. Full Access is not used for anything else.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card.

On the Mac download

  • Speech recognition and translation run on your Mac. Audio, transcripts and captions are not sent to us.
  • If you turn on a cloud speech or AI model and enter your own API key, audio or text goes to that provider under its terms. This is off by default.
  • There are no accounts, analytics or telemetry.
  • The license key is checked on your Mac. The app never sends it anywhere.
  • The app checks GitHub for new releases. GitHub sees your IP address when it does.
  • Feedback you choose to post on GitHub is public.

When you buy

Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing address and payment details to process the order, under Paddle's privacy policy. We never see your card number.

From Paddle we receive your email address and the order details, so we can send your key and help you if something goes wrong.

Key delivery

A small service on Cloudflare signs your key and emails it to you through Resend. It stores your email address, the key and the order number, so a repeated notification from Paddle never sends a second key and we can resend a lost one.

Support email

If you write to support@local-host.ai, we keep the conversation to answer you and to help with later questions.

Websites

  • local-host.ai and chrisswimlee.com are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses.
  • There are no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • chrisswimlee.com loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address.
  • Opening the checkout loads Paddle's script, which can set cookies needed for payment and fraud checks.

How long we keep it

Order emails and keys are kept as long as you might need your key resent, and as long as tax and accounting rules require. Support emails are kept until they are no longer needed.

Your choices

Ask us at support@local-host.ai to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. For payment records, Paddle is the controller, and you can also ask Paddle directly. We do not sell personal data.

Children

Connecting Captions is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

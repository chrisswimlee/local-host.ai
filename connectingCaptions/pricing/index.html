Licensing questions

Which license do I need?
The Mac download is free until the App Store app is available. Any Mac a school, company, or other organization owns can add a work license.
How many Macs does the free download cover?
The current Mac download has no key and no seat cap. Share it under GPLv3. Work licenses are counted per Mac.
What does the free download include?
The full Mac app, until the App Store version is available. App Store unlocks, once they are on sale, stay unlocked on the devices that use your Apple Account.
Does Listen ask for a key?
Not in the current Mac download. It stays free until the App Store app is available.
Does my voice leave the Mac?
No, unless someone turns on a cloud speech model. Speech and translation run on the device.
When does the Mac app stop being free?
When the App Store app is available. Until then, download the zip and press Listen.

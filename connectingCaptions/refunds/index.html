Refund policy

The full app is free to try for 14 days before you buy, so please use the trial to make sure it fits. Questions go to support@local-host.ai.

Last updated 26 October 2026

Sales are final

Because you can try everything first, purchases of a personal key or a work license are not refundable, except as described below.

If a key does not work

If your key does not unlock the app and we cannot fix it, you get a full refund. Email support@local-host.ai with your order number or the email you bought with.

Your legal rights

Where the law gives you a right to cancel a purchase, for example in the EU, the UK, South Korea or Canada, that right still applies, and Paddle, which processed your order, handles those requests. Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under consumer law.

Paddle may also refund a purchase at its own discretion under its refund policy. You can reach Paddle through paddle.net.

Work license renewals

A yearly work license renews unless you tell us before the renewal date that you want to stop. Write to us before that date to avoid the next charge.

App Store

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

After a refund

The key stops being yours to use. Please remove it from Connecting Captions.
